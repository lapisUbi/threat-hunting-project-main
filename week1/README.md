## Why this is needed

Before collecting data in week 2 I needed to understand the basic words: what an IOC is, what a TTP is, what types of intelligence exist. The classification helped to pick the focus. For banks in Kazakhstan I chose ransomware groups, because they attack finance more than anyone else and some of them already had victims in Kazakhstan (see week 2).
